### New
- **Duets:** duet charts stay intact, both singers are shown, and you edit one at a time. Make any song a duet in the editor
- **YouTube:** paste a link instead of uploading a file, use the video's subtitles as lyrics, and export the video
- **Export to a folder:** choose a location and get a song folder ready for the game, or a USDB .txt to upload
- **Better vocal separation:** an optional slower model, much cleaner where voices overlap
- **Editor:** Set START, golden rap notes, syllable search, warnings for overlapping notes, and a menu to hide toolbar tools

### Better charts
- Lyrics are aligned directly to the vocals: on six test songs, misplaced syllables dropped from 57 % to 7 %
- A new note model gets more pitches right, and low voices stay low
- Notes start on the sung vowel, long notes keep their length, and melodies within a syllable become ~ notes
- English and many more languages are split into syllables as they are sung

### Easier to use
- "Which song is this?" proposes artist, title and language after an upload
- Step 2 compares lyrics from several sources side by side
- Generating and extracting vocals show real progress
- The app updates itself: this window is new too

### Fixed
- Exported files follow the UltraStar format and USDB's conventions
- Importing charts from USDB gives the right pitches
- Many fixes for Windows and Intel Macs
