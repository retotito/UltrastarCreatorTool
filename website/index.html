Four steps to a finished song

From an audio file to an UltraStar chart you can sing in the game.

Step 1

Upload

Choose a file or paste a YouTube link. The app proposes artist, title and language, and separates the vocals from the music.

Step 2

Lyrics

Compare lyrics side by side from a published source, the video's subtitles, or what Whisper hears in the vocals. Then generate the chart.

Step 3

Editor

Check every note against the waveform. Move, split and join notes, loop a difficult line, slow it down, or sing along with your microphone.

Step 4

Export

Get a song folder ready for the game, with cover, background and video, or a .txt in the format USDB uses.

New in 5.0

Duets

Duet charts stay intact and both singers are shown. You edit one at a time, and any song can become a duet.

YouTube links

Paste a link instead of uploading a file, use its subtitles as lyrics, and export the video.

Better charts

Lyrics are aligned directly to the vocals: on six test songs, misplaced syllables dropped from 57 % to 7 %.

Better vocal separation

An optional slower model is much cleaner where voices overlap.

The first start

The app downloads its AI models, about 3.5 GB. It isn't code-signed yet, so your system warns you the first time:

macOS: drag the app to Applications and open it. When macOS blocks it, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

Windows: when SmartScreen warns you, click More info, then Run anyway.

Coming from a version before 5.0?

Export the songs you want to keep as a ZIP, close and uninstall the old app, then install this one.
