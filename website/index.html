Create UltraStar songs with the help of AI

Four steps to a finished song

From a song on your computer or on YouTube to an UltraStar song you can sing.

Step 1

Upload

Upload a song or paste a YouTube link. Then extract the vocals from the song.

Step 2

Lyrics

Search for the song's lyrics or generate them from the vocals, then double-check and edit them.

Step 3

Editor

After the AI has generated the notes, fine-tune them by hand.

Step 4

Export

Save the finished song as a folder, ready to sing in the game, or as a file to share on USDB, the UltraStar song library.

New in 5.0

Duets

Duet songs stay intact and both singers are shown. You edit one at a time, and any song can become a duet.

YouTube links

Paste a link instead of uploading a file, use its subtitles as lyrics, and export the video.

Better results

Lyrics are aligned directly to the vocals: on six test songs, misplaced syllables dropped from 57 % to 7 %.

Better vocal separation

An optional slower model is much cleaner where voices overlap.

Export for the game or USDB

A song folder wherever you want it, or a .txt ready to upload.

The first start

The app downloads its AI models, about 3.5 GB. It isn't code-signed yet, so your system warns you the first time:

macOS: drag the app to Applications and open it. When macOS blocks it, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

Windows: when SmartScreen warns you, click More info, then Run anyway.

Coming from a version before 5.0?

Export the songs you want to keep as a ZIP, close and uninstall the old app, then install this one.
