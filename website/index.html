4 steps to create an UltraStar song

  1. Upload

    Upload a song or paste a YouTube link. Then extract the vocals from the song.

  2. Lyrics

    Search for the song's lyrics or generate them from the vocals, then double-check and edit them.

  3. Editor

    After the AI has generated the notes, fine-tune them by hand.

  4. Export

    Save the finished song as a folder, ready to sing in the game, or as a file to share on USDB, the UltraStar song library.

Your download has started

The app is not signed by Apple or Microsoft (that costs a yearly fee), so your computer asks once whether you trust it. Here is how to say yes:

  1. Open the downloaded .dmg and drag UltrastarCreatorTool into Applications.
  2. Open it. macOS blocks it the first time, so close the message.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.
  4. Confirm. From now on it opens normally.
  1. Start the downloaded .msi.
  2. If a blue window says "Windows protected your PC", click More info.
  3. Click Run anyway and follow the installer.

The first start downloads the AI models, about 3.5 GB. After that, the app offers new versions by itself. Your songs and models stay.

Coming from a version before 5.0? Export the songs you want to keep as a ZIP, close and uninstall the old app, then install this one.
